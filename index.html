Services

Beauty · Fashion · Jewelry · Lifestyle

Gifting & PR collaborations

I’m early in my creator journey, and I put real care into every piece I make.

For beauty, fashion, jewelry and lifestyle brands: if your product fits my world, I’ll build a considered, on-brand feature around it — concept, styling, filming and edit from one point of view. Formats and timing are agreed with you up front.

  1. I

    Unboxing & first impressions

    An honest first look — packaging, details and my real first reaction, shot in soft editorial light.

  2. II

    Get ready with me

    Your product worked into a full get-ready routine, from first step to the finished look.

  3. III

    One piece, three ways

    A single piece styled three ways — day, evening and night out — to show its range.

  4. IV

    Cinematic storytime

    A 15–30s voiceover or storytime cut that gives your product a narrative, not just a close-up.
